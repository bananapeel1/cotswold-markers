For businesses on the Cotswold Way

Walkers are about to tap the posts. Here's how they'll find you.

TrailTap puts small plates on the Cotswold Way's waymark posts. A walker taps one with their phone, or scans its code, and a page opens: the story of where they're standing, and the nearest food, water, toilets and places to rest. Free for walkers, nothing to download.

Here's how it works, in three steps.

What's included

  • Right under the map
    On the plates around Broadway, your card is the first thing after the map: above What's Next, Nearby and the stories.
  • Directions to your door
    One tap opens Google Maps with the walking route to you.
  • Your own line
    A sentence in your words, changed whenever you like: today's lunch, an event, a walkers' welcome.
  • A website button
    Your website, booking page or menu. You choose what it opens.
  • Your Nearby card
    The answer to the walker's question, with the distance to you.
  • Your events in Look Out For
    Seasonal events listed alongside the area's own.
  • An offer, if you want one
    Optional. Some businesses like a walkers' welcome; you don't need one.
  • A monthly report
    Taps, people who saw your card, directions and button taps.

Why it works

Direct marketing, on the doorstep.

Most marketing reaches people far from your door, long before they're ready to decide. TrailTap reaches them on the path, nearby, while they're deciding.

Right person, right place, right moment

A walker taps a plate when they're deciding what to do next. Your business is right under the map on that page, close by. Very little marketing reaches a customer that close to your door.

Straight to you, no middleman

No algorithm and no booking-site commission. One tap opens Google Maps with the route to you.

Your words, whenever you like

Change your line for wet days, quiet weekdays, the lavender season or an event.

You'll see what it did

A short report every month: taps near you, people who saw your card, directions and button taps.

Goodwill that lasts

Named as a founding partner of something useful for walkers and for the village.

Nothing to install

We set it up. No app, screen or login for you or your staff.

Who's walking past

People on foot, with needs right now.
