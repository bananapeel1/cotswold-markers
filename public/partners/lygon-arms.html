TrailTap × The Lygon Arms

Walkers end their first day at your door.

Broadway is about six miles from the start of the Cotswold Way, and for many walkers it's the first night's stop. From 15 March 2027, the TrailTap pilot puts 30 plates on the Way around Broadway. Each one shows walkers where to eat, drink and sleep. Here's how The Lygon Arms could be the first thing they see.

What's included

  • Right under the map
    On the plates around Broadway, your card is the first thing after the map: above What's Next, Discovery and the stories.
  • Directions to your door
    One tap opens Google Maps with the walking route to you.
  • Your own line
    A sentence in your words, changed whenever you like: today's lunch, an event, a walkers' welcome.
  • A Book a table button
    Your website, booking page or menu. You choose what it opens.
  • Your Discovery card
    The answer to the walker's question, with the distance to you.
  • Your events in Look Out For
    Seasonal events listed alongside the area's own.
  • An offer, if you want one
    Optional. Some businesses like a walkers' welcome; you don't need one.
  • A monthly report
    Taps, people who saw your card, directions and button taps.
Why it works for The Lygon Arms

Direct marketing, on your doorstep.

Most marketing reaches people far from your door, long before they're ready to decide. TrailTap reaches them on the path, nearby, while they're deciding.

Guests and diners already in the village

Walkers arrive hungry and need somewhere to stay. Your card is right under the map as they come down into Broadway.

Bookings straight to you

Your button opens your own booking page, with no booking-site commission in between.

Your line, your timing

A walkers' welcome in spring, a midweek dinner, afternoon tea on a wet day. Change it whenever you like.

A count you can trust
