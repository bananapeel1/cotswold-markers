The kit shop on the Way, found at the moment it's needed.

Six miles in, walkers coming down into Broadway find out what isn't working: boots that rub, socks that slip, a jacket that leaks. The Epicentre is on the High Street, on the Cotswold Way itself. From 1 February 2027, the TrailTap pilot puts 30 plates on the Way around Broadway, each one showing walkers what's near.

TrailTap in a nutshell

A plate on the post. A page for that spot.

Small plates on waymark posts along the Cotswold Way. A walker taps one with their phone or scans its code, and a page opens: where they are, what's next, and what's nearby: food, water, toilets, beds and shops, with walking directions. Free for walkers, nothing to download.

  • With permissionWorking with the Cotswold Way Association and Cotswolds National Landscape, with their permission to use the posts.
  • Only on existing postsPlates go on posts that are already there. Nothing new on the landscape.
  • Checked storiesThe stories on each page are checked against published sources.

What's included

  • Right under the map
    On the plates around Broadway, your card is the first thing after the map: above What's Next, Nearby and the stories.
  • Directions to your door
    One tap opens Google Maps with the walking route to you.
  • Your own line
    A sentence in your words, changed whenever you like: today's lunch, an event, a walkers' welcome.
  • A Website button
    Your website, booking page or menu. You choose what it opens.
  • Your Nearby card
    The answer to the walker's question, with the distance to you.
  • Your events in Look Out For
    Seasonal events listed alongside the area's own.
  • An offer, if you want one
    Optional. Some businesses like a walkers' welcome; you don't need one.
  • A monthly report
    Taps, people who saw your card, directions and button taps.

Why it works for The Epicentre

Direct marketing, on your doorstep.

Most marketing reaches people far from your door, long before they're ready to decide. TrailTap reaches them on the path, nearby, while they're deciding.

Sold at the point of need

A walker with a blister or a wet jacket doesn't browse, they buy. Your card tells them you're on their way.
