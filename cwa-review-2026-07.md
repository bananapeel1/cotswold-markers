# Cotswold Way content expansion — verification report for CWA review

**Prepared:** 28 July 2026 · **Scope:** the Cotswolds Way Association's suggestions — toilets split into accessible/general, pharmacy and emergency-medical locations, and historic sites (Roman, Civil War, medieval, burial mounds, tumuli).

Every entry below was verified this month against a named external source — council and NHS pages, operating trusts, English Heritage / National Trust, and Historic England's National Heritage List (NHLE). Nothing was published from memory or guesswork. Full row-by-row detail with source URLs is in the three companion spreadsheets:

- `toilet-audit-2026-07.csv` — 13 corrected + 12 new toilet entries
- `health-audit-2026-07.csv` — 18 pharmacies + 7 emergency/urgent-care facilities
- `historic-audit-2026-07.csv` — 49 historic sites with NHLE numbers and access status

## 1. Toilets (now 25 entries, all tagged accessible / standard / unknown)

- Every real facility now records whether an **accessible toilet** is provided and whether a **RADAR key** is needed (Chipping Campden, Painswick, Dursley, Wotton and Stonehouse all require one; the Bath council blocks explicitly don't).
- **Corrections found:** Wotton's pin was ~460 m wrong (the block is on Rope Walk); Crickley Hill's pin was ~250 m off; Broadway's toilets are in Church Close, not Church Street; seven sets of opening hours didn't match the councils' published hours.
- **New entries include three Changing Places facilities** (Broadway Activity Park, Dursley War Memorial Recreation Ground — opened late 2025, Sydney Gardens Bath) and a "no public toilets on Cleeve Common" warning to match the existing Cooper's Hill and Coaley Peak warnings, both of which we re-confirmed.

**For CWA ground-checking (conflicting or unverifiable sources):**
1. **St Mary's Street, Painswick** — the parish council's website says a disabled-suitable facility exists; the Great British Public Toilet Map recorded it removed in 2018 ("now an art gallery"). A five-minute local check settles it.
2. **Winchcombe Back Lane** — no source confirms an accessible cubicle; worth checking.
3. **Fish Hill picnic site** — Worcestershire CC lists a disabled visitors' toilet but a visitor review disputes it, and no opening hours are published anywhere.
4. **Dursley WMRG** — brand-new facility; opening hours not yet published (Town Council 01453 547758).
5. **Hailes Abbey** — visitor toilets exist but the abbey currently opens only the first weekend of each month; we left it out as unreliable for walkers.

## 2. Pharmacies and emergency medical (25 entries)

- **Pharmacies:** every trail town that has one is covered (verified via NHS.uk profiles): Chipping Campden, Broadway, Winchcombe, Painswick, Stonehouse ×2, Dursley ×3, Cam, Wotton-under-Edge, Weston and central Bath ×4 — plus nearest-option entries for the Cleeve Hill leg (Prestbury Road, Cheltenham) and the Sodbury leg (Chipping Sodbury). Ten villages confirmed to have **no** pharmacy, so the gaps are deliberate, not oversights.
- **The safety-critical fact:** the only 24-hour consultant-led A&Es serving the Way are **Gloucestershire Royal** and **RUH Bath**. **Cheltenham General is consultant-led 8am–8pm only** — overnight it is a nurse-led minor injuries unit with no ambulances and no children. This is stated prominently in its entry.
- Minor injuries units at Stroud, Dursley (Vale), Yate and Moreton-in-Marsh (nearest to the Campden start) are included with their "call NHS 111 first" guidance.

## 3. Historic sites (49 entries)

- **Coverage:** 27 prehistoric (long barrows, tumuli, hillforts — Belas Knap, Hetty Pegler's Tump, Nympsfield, Uley Bury, Crickley Hill, Cleeve Cloud and 21 more), 4 Roman (incl. the Roman Baths and the Uley shrines), 9 medieval (Hailes Abbey, Sudeley, Horton Court, Bath Abbey…), 4 Civil War (Battle of Lansdown registered battlefield, Grenville's Monument, Old Campden House, St Mary's Painswick), 5 other.
- **Trust anchor:** 45 of 49 carry their Historic England NHLE list entry number in the data, so any entry can be independently checked in seconds. The four without (Dover's Hill, Barrow Wake, Peak Camp, Kelston Round Hill) are flagged with alternative sources, and legends are labelled as such (the Battle of Deorham link to Dyrham Camp is stated as documentary tradition, exactly as the NHLE does; Kelston's "tumulus" is flagged as unverified tradition).
- **Access honesty:** every site is classed open access (27) / visible from trail (10) / paid attraction (6) / no public access (6), with the restriction repeated in the visible description so walkers are never sent off rights of way.
- **Time-sensitive findings:** Great Witcombe Roman Villa **and its car park are currently closed** (English Heritage, checked 28 July) — our entry warns against detouring; Hailes Abbey is currently pre-booked tours on the first weekend of each month only; Hetty Pegler's Tump interior is currently open (bring a torch).
